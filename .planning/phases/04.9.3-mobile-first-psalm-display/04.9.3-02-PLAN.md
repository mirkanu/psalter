Refactor `NotationRenderer` from purely-uncontrolled to optionally-controlled, add
a `chromeless` mode that suppresses its internal controlBar + fullscreen entry,
and lower the mobile default `--staff-base-size` from 24 to 13 per UI-SPEC.

states live inside `NotationRenderer`, hidden behind its own controlBar.

component remains usable on `/study` and `/tunes/[id]` in its original
uncontrolled form.

Output:

  `chromeless` + the mobile-13 default. All existing call sites continue
  to compile and render identically because the new props are optional.
