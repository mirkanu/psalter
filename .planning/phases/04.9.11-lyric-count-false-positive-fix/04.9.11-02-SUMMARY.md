**One-liner:** Fixed abcjs trailing-note lyric gap by replacing `_` padding with middle dot (U+00B7) in `padWLineToNoteCount`, achieving 139/139 (100%) active-tune pass rate.

## Objective

Fix the root cause of lyric-under-note misalignment that caused 85 of 139 active tunes to fail the staff alignment checker (Plan 01 baseline after false-positive fix).

## What Was Built

### Task 1 — `padWLineToNoteCount` padding fix (commit `346527d`)

Changed the padding token in `NotationRenderer.tsx` `padWLineToNoteCount` from `_` (ABC melisma hold) to `·` (U+00B7 middle dot).

**Root cause:** `_` in ABC `w:` lines is interpreted by abcjs as `{skip: true, to: 'slur'}`, producing `{syllable: "", divider: " "}` — an empty `<tspan>` in the DOM. The UAT checker's `textContent.trim() !== ""` test correctly identifies these as uncovered note heads. Notes padded with `_` always appeared as `trailingEmpty` regardless of how many syllables the lyric provided.

**Why middle dot works:** `·` is not a whitespace character (not matched by `/\s+/`) and is not in abcjs's special-character list. It gets stored as a syllable word `{syllable: "·", divider: " "}` and the DOM `<tspan>` renders with non-empty `textContent`. The checker counts it as covered.

**Why NBSP failed:** JavaScript's `/\s+/` regex matches non-breaking space. abcjs's `parseCommon.strip` uses `/\s+/` internally — NBSP tokens are silently dropped, producing the same empty tspan problem.

**File modified:** `src/components/notation/NotationRenderer.tsx` — `padWLineToNoteCount` function, line ~775.
