## DCM Psalm Chosen

```sql
SELECT pv.psalter_number, t.name FROM psalm_versions pv
  JOIN psalm_version_tunes pvt ON pvt.psalm_version_id = pv.id
  JOIN tunes t ON t.id = pvt.tune_id
  WHERE t.double_length = true AND pv.lyrics_structured IS NOT NULL
  ORDER BY pv.psalter_number LIMIT 5;
```
