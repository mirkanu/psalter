## Bug-Fix Iteration (Task 3 deviation, pre-approved)

The Task 2 dry-run surfaced two algorithmic shortfalls. User pre-approved fixing both before the LIVE write.

### Bug 1 — `w:` lyric lines miscounted as music lines

**Found during:** Task 3 dry-run inspection of id=34 (St. Peter), id=64 (Martyrdom).
**Issue:** ABC `w:` lyric fields contain `|` for syllable-to-barline alignment. The original "line contains `|`" detector treated those as music lines, causing markers to be inserted between a `w:` line and the next stanza of lyrics instead of between music staves.
**Fix:** Filter ABC info-field lines (`^\s*[A-Za-z]:`) out of the music-line index. The detection now correctly excludes `w:`, `V:`, `M:`, and any future single-letter info headers.
**Files modified:** `scripts/annotate-phrase-breaks.ts` (added `isInfoFieldLine` helper, line 50).
**Regression tests:** 2 new (st-peter-like fixture with embedded `w:` lines; V:-header fixture).
**Commit:** `bd944ca`

### Bug 2 — Single-physical-line music bodies cannot be split by line-based heuristic

**Found during:** Task 3 dry-run; 13 tunes reported `annotation unsafe`, including id=29 (Old 100th), id=162 (Effingham), id=86 (Walton), plus Abbeyville, St. Kilda, Praetorius, Jackson, Kathrine, Dunfermline, St. Lawrence, University, Ballerma, Corona.
**Issue:** ~13 Airtable-sourced tunes have their entire music body on one physical ABC line. The line-based algorithm could never insert a marker in that case, violating must_have ("every eligible row has ≥1 marker").
**Fix:** Added a Path B fallback. When music-line count < n but is exactly 1, count internal `|` barlines in that line (excluding leading bar and trailing `|]` end-of-piece marker), pick (n−1) positions evenly spaced across them, and splice in `\n% PHRASE_BREAK\n` mid-line. Because `splitOnPhraseBreaks` uses `/m` flag, the marker lives on its own line and round-trips correctly.
**Files modified:** `scripts/annotate-phrase-breaks.ts` (Path B block, lines 90–140).
**Regression tests:** 4 new (single-line CM → 1 marker, single-line DCM → 3 markers, idempotent re-run on already-marked single-line body, too-few-barlines guard).
**Commit:** `bd944ca`
