<objective>
Item (c): surface verse numbers in the inline-Staff `w:` lyric lines. Today
`mapCycleToPhraseSyllableLines` builds `w:` text from `Line.text` (via `syllabifyForAbc`) and
silently DROPS `Line.bibleVerseRef`. Per locked decision 3 (and MOBILE-06), the fix is to glue
the plain verse digit to the first syllable of each verse-opening line — the exact convention
`serialiseLyrics` already uses (`${bibleVerseRef}${text}`). A `<sup>` is technically impossible
inside a `w:` line (abcjs renders `w:` as its own SVG `<text>` nodes — no JSX injection point,
RESEARCH Anti-Patterns), so digit-glue is the correct and only viable approach.

Purpose: Inline-Staff singers see the same verse markers they see in lyrics-only/split-leaf.
Output: A one-function change in the pure `stanza-cycles.ts` transform, fully unit-tested.
</objective>
