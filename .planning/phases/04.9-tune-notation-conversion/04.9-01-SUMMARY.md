## Decisions Made

- **Image resizing via sharp:** 102 of 168 solfège images are >3.75MB raw (>5MB base64), which is Anthropic's hard limit. Added `prepareImageBase64()` using `sharp` to scale+compress to 85% JPEG quality when oversized. Without this, ~60% of tunes would fail with a `BadRequestError`.
- **postProcessAbc() pipeline:** LLM outputs three recurring error patterns that cause abcjs warnings: (1) `F#` instead of `^F` sharp notation (~5% of tunes), (2) tonic sol-fa `:- ` beat separators leaking into ABC music lines (~10%), (3) solfège note names (`r`, `l`, `s`, `m`, `t`) appearing as isolated tokens in ABC note sequences (~10%). The combined post-processor raises success rate from ~65% to ~97%.
- **First X:1 block extraction:** LLM occasionally outputs two or three attempts separated by blank lines with a new `X:1` header. Only the first is extracted.
- **API key fallback:** DEBATES_ANTHROPIC_API_KEY and YNAB_ANTHROPIC_API_KEY both exhausted credits mid-run. A PSALTER_ANTHROPIC_API_KEY with sufficient credits is needed in `/home/services/.env.production`.
