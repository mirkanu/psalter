## Conclusion vs. the Phase 15.2 localhost verdict

Phase 15.2's `lighthouse-bench.mjs` reported localhost medians of 0.68 /
0.69 / 0.45. **All three of those are now demonstrably VPS-CPU-contaminated
measurements, NOT real-world performance.**

The Phase 15.2 evidence file already explained why: the VPS runs three
Claude processes (this one + the user's main session + a haiku subagent),
the playwright-daemon, gsddashboard, and pm2 — load average 2.56 on a ~1
CPU equivalent. Under those conditions, Lighthouse's `benchmarkIndex` flux
was 477–1253 (vs Google infrastructure's stable value), and identical code
on this VPS scores ±6 points just from run-to-run noise.

What this means for POLISH-03:
- **The 90+ gate IS met on `/` and `/psalms/23` (96 and 100).**
- **`/psalms` is at a perfect 100.**
- The "framework floor" hypothesis from `15.2-FINAL-ANALYSIS.md` was
  partly an artifact of VPS load. Real-world TBT is 30–90 ms (well
  inside the "good" band of <200 ms), not the 1000-2000 ms we saw locally.

POLISH-03 should be flipped from `[ ]` to `[x] complete` on the strength
of these external measurements. The post-measurement clean-up Phase 15.2
shipped (GlobalSearchClient + FeedbackModalClient wrappers, scripts/lighthouse-bench.mjs
harness, next.config.ts qualities self-consistency, useSession display-gate)
remains in place — these are objective bundle wins even if they can't
be measured precisely on this VPS.

## How this was retrieved

The daemon-side Playwright session (port 3099):
1. Loaded each PSI share URL (or for /psalms/23, submitted via the
   search form because the share-ID didn't survive the post-test
   linker) and waited for the score panel + metric cards to render
   (text-soup check for `First Contentful Paint` + `Largest Contentful
   Paint` + `Total Blocking Time`, polled every 1.5s up to 25s).
2. Read `document.body.innerText` and DOM-traversed for the four
   category badges (Performance / Accessibility / Best Practices / SEO)
   and the seven standard metric labels.
3. Returned the parsed values.

The naive top-of-file line parsing initially mistook the copyright
year "2026" / "Slow 4G throttling" caption deltas as a SEO score of 2;
this was corrected by reading the full text dump and locating the
"100" SEO badge header line.
