## Area 1: Notation display in Overview

**Question asked:** How should live abcjs notation relate to the Staff Score JPG in the Recommended Tune section?

**User's direction (freeform):** Significantly rethought the layout. The congregation mostly can't read notes — abcjs with lyrics overlaid is the right display (helps them learn). Lyrics + notation should be BELOW the tabs, always visible. Toggle to hide notation and show plain lyrics. Staff/Solfège toggle with localStorage memory. Desktop: SoundCloud to the right of lyrics/notation. Mobile: SoundCloud inside a "Tune" tab.

**Follow-up:** Clarified mobile tab structure and Overview tab content.
- Mobile: 8 tabs — "Tune" prepended. Tune tab has SoundCloud + tune metadata.
- Overview tab: psalm metadata only (no lyrics, no SoundCloud).

**Decision:** D-01 through D-07 (see CONTEXT.md).

## Area 2: Stanza rendering under staff

**Question asked:** All stanzas at once (hymnal layout) or one stanza at a time with Prev/Next?

**User's direction (freeform):** Neither — up to 4 stanzas at once (one score rendering with 4 `w:` lines underneath), with Prev/Next to advance through groups of 4. Rejected the idea of repeating the score for every stanza.

**Decision:** D-08 — stanza groups of 4, one score rendering, Prev/Next pages through groups.

## Area 3: Mobile layout priorities

**Options presented:** Controls above score vs all controls below; scroll-with-page vs fixed height.

**Selected:** Controls above score; scroll with page.

**Decision:** D-12 — toggles above score, Prev/Next below, notation scrolls with page.
