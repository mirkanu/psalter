**RED phase:** Added 5 new test cases to `scripts/annotate-phrase-breaks.test.ts`:

- `insertPhraseBreaks` with splitPoints=[8,14,22] must produce 3 markers

Tests failed as expected (adjacent markers produced, only 2 phrases split).

**GREEN phase:** Replaced "Path NH" in `insertPhraseBreaks` (lines ~146-166) with a character/token-level tokenizer:
- Joins all body lines into a single string and walks character-by-character
- Detects info-field lines (w:, V:, K:, M:) at line start and skips them
- Handles grace notes `{..}`, annotations `"..."`, decorations `!...!` (skip, no count)
- Handles chords `[..]` as 1 note head
- Handles ties `-` by consuming the next note without incrementing count
- Handles bare notes (optional accidental + note letter + octave + duration)
- Handles rests z/x/Z without counting
- Collects splice positions, then inserts `\n% PHRASE_BREAK\n` descending order
- Collapses `\n\n+` to `\n` to ensure idempotency

All 39 tests pass (34 existing + 5 new). The `line.includes('|')` check is gone from the noteHeadSplitPoints path (count = 0). Exported signature unchanged.

### Task 05-03: App Restart + Playwright Sweep

**Additional deviation fix discovered:** When phrase bodies span multiple ABC text lines (e.g. Crimond phrase 3: `=b4 |\n=b2c'4a2 | a2b2a2g4`), the `cleanedBody` in `NotationRenderer.tsx` preserved the internal newline. When pushed into `parts.join('\n')`, abcjs saw 2 music lines = 2 sub-staves instead of 1.

**Fix applied to `NotationRenderer.tsx`** (Rule 1 deviation): replaced simple `.join('\n')` with a reducer that merges consecutive music lines (non-info-field lines) with a space join. Info-field lines (V:, K:, etc.) are kept on their own lines.
