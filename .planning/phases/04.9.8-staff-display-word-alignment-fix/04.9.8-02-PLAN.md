Three coordinated changes deployed in sequence to fix staff word alignment:
1. Upgrade `insertPhraseBreaks` to use note-head counting and run the migration against all CM/LM/SM tunes (writes 3 PHRASE_BREAKs per tune).

Therefore the tasks are ordered: (a) upgrade the script, (b) dry-run + human checkpoint, (c) live migration, (d) phrasesForMeter change + z2 fix, (e) deploy/restart.
