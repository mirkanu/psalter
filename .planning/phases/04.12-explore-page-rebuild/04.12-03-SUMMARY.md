## Component Contracts

**QuotedInNT** (`'use client'`) — receives `NTEntry[]`, renders one shadcn Collapsible per entry. Trigger shows sub_topic label + verse count badge + chevron. Content lists psalm verse links + italic quotation text.

**HeidelbergCatechism** (`'use client'`) — receives `CatechismRow[]`, renders one Collapsible per question number. External anchor hrefs are guarded: `row.url.startsWith('http') ? row.url : \`https://\${row.url}\`` prevents same-origin redirect hijack (T-04.12-06 mitigated). All links carry `target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"`.

**MessianicByTopic** (server) — receives `MessianicTopic[]`, renders single "Messianic" label + 2/3/4-column pill grid linking to `/explore/naves/[slug]`. Contains local `slugify()` copy.

**ExploreAnchorNav** (`'use client'`) — no props. Sticky bar at `top-[64px]`, 6 section anchors. `IntersectionObserver` with `rootMargin: '-10% 0px -85% 0px'` (anti-flicker band) sets active section. Active item: `border-b-2 border-primary -mb-px font-semibold`.
