## Decisions Made

- `wLineForSyllables` is defined inside the `useMemo` body rather than as a `useCallback` — it must close over `solfegeVoices`, `tuneMeter`, and the loop variable `i`, all of which are already in the `useMemo` dep array. A separate `useCallback` would be redundant.
- `solfegeOcrText` is optional in `NotationRendererProps` (`?: string | null`) so tune-detail page callers (which don't have solfège data) don't need changes.
- Type cast in PsalmTabs is needed because `activeTune` is an ad-hoc union type; the cast is safe since both branches now include `solfegeOcrText`.
