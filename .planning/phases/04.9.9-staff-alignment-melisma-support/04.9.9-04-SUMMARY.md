## Root Cause of 102 Failures

The failures are NOT from:
- Missing solfège OCR data (all 102 failing tunes have OCR data)
- Failing melisma heuristics (buildWLineFromSolfa converges for all)

The failures ARE from:
- Phase 04.9.8 PHRASE_BREAK markers placed at ABC text-line boundaries instead of at exact note-head count offsets (character-level)
- When PHRASE_BREAK is in the wrong position, a phrase contains too many notes (e.g., 15 for a CM phrase that should have 8)
- A w: line with `_` tokens cannot correct for wrong note counts in the phrase

### Example: Psalm 3 (Abbeyville, CM)
- Failure: `abcjs-l3: noteCount=15, notesWithLyric=6, trailingEmpty=9`
- Expected for CM phrase 3: ~8 notes (last "I'll not want" line)
- 7 extra notes from adjacent phrase incorrectly folded in

### Example: Psalm 100 (Old 100th, LM)
- Failure: `abcjs-l3: noteCount=24, notesWithLyric=8, trailingEmpty=16`
- Old 100th ABC has 3 explicit stanza repetitions of the last phrase (separate music lines, not w: stanza stacking)
- Current system emits one w: line; stanzas 2+3's music lines have no w: lines
