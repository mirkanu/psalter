## Root Cause of Remaining 102 Failures

The 102 remaining failures are NOT caused by missing solfège OCR data or failing melisma heuristics. All 102 failing tunes have `solfege_ocr_text` in the DB and the melisma pipeline is active for them.

**Root cause:** The PHRASE_BREAK markers in `abc_notation` are at ABC text-line boundaries, not at exact note-head count offsets within lines (the Phase 04.9.8 `insertPhraseBreaks` bug). When a PHRASE_BREAK is at the wrong position, the phrase contains more notes than expected (e.g., 15 notes for a CM phrase that should have 8), and the w: line cannot align regardless of melisma tokens.

Evidence: psalm 3 (Abbeyville, CM) fails with `abcjs-l3: noteCount=15, notesWithLyric=6`. The last CM phrase should have ~8 notes; the extra 7 are from the adjacent phrase being incorrectly folded in due to PHRASE_BREAK misplacement.

Example failing tuple: psalm 23 (Crimond), psalm 3 (Abbeyville), psalm 100 (Old 100th, LM).

## Residual Known Variance

None of the residual failures are from "no OCR data" variance. All 102 failures require the PHRASE_BREAK fix from Phase 04.9.8.

**Specific investigation of Old 100th (LM) — psalm 100:**
- `abcjs-l3` shows 24 note positions. Expected: 8 for one LM phrase.
- Cause: The Old 100th ABC notation has 3 explicit stanza repetitions of the last phrase appended as separate music lines (not as additional w: stanzas). The current system emits one w: line for this staff row (for stanza 1 only), leaving stanzas 2 and 3's music lines with no w: lines.
- This is a separate structural issue from PHRASE_BREAK positions: the ABC notation encodes multi-stanza repetition differently from the `w:` stacking model the renderer assumes.
