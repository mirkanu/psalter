### Gaps Summary

The phase successfully delivered the melisma infrastructure: `getPassingPositions` correctly detects dot-pair passing notes; `buildWLineFromSolfa` correctly emits `_` tokens; the full pipeline is wired from DB through NotationRenderer. Unit tests are comprehensive and all pass. 34 additional psalms now align correctly (48 vs 14 baseline).

However, the primary phase goal canary — Crimond (psalm 23) phrase 4 — still fails. The root cause is a mismatch between the note-event count that `buildWLineFromSolfa` computes for phrase 4 (based on CM split-point boundaries = 10 events for events[22..31]) and the actual note count rendered in the ABC staff (12 notes, because PHRASE_BREAK in the stored `abc_notation` is mispositioned from Phase 04.9.8). The melisma pipeline cannot compensate for wrong PHRASE_BREAK positions — it can only assign `_` tokens within the note count it is given by the phrase boundary map.

Both ROADMAP success criteria 4 and 5 are FAILED. Success criterion 4 is a blocking canary — it names Crimond/psalm 23 explicitly as the test case. The sweep confirms it remains broken.

**Root cause of gap:** The PHRASE_BREAK positions in stored `abc_notation` are at ABC text-line character boundaries, not at exact note-head count offsets (Phase 04.9.8 `insertPhraseBreaks` bug, documented in 04.9.8-VERIFICATION.md). Fixing this requires rewriting `insertPhraseBreaks` with character-level tokenization and re-running the DB migration — work that is outside Phase 04.9.9's scope but is a prerequisite for the phase goal.
