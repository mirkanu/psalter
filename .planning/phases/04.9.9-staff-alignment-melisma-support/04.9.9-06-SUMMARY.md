## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Solfège parser Amen-stripping truncated Stracathro and Rimington ABC**
- **Found during:** Task 1 (during investigation of why all 7 tunes showed only 14–19 note heads)
- **Issue:** The solfège-to-ABC parser's Amen-stripping logic treated the midpoint `||` separator as end-of-tune and discarded everything after it, including the second half of the tune. Both Stracathro and Rimington had complete solfège OCR data (all 4 phrases present) but their generated ABC was cut in half.
- **Fix:** Created `scripts/fix-stracathro-rimington.ts` which appends `||` to soprano solfège for both tunes (providing a valid terminator), regenerates ABC via `solFaToAbc`, then re-runs annotation to place 3 PHRASE_BREAK markers. DB updated live.
- **Files modified:** `scripts/fix-stracathro-rimington.ts` (created)
