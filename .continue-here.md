- 2 are dot-pair passing notes (f.,r → r is passing) — detectable from sol-fa OCR text
- 4 are from 3-slot cells (m:r:r, m:f:m etc.) — not explicitly marked as passing in sol-fa

### The fix strategy (hybrid approach, zero AI cost)

**Step 1 — Dot-pair passing notes from sol-fa OCR text (already in DB)**
Modify `parseVoiceLine` in `src/lib/solfege-parser.ts` to tag dot-pair second tokens as `passing: true`. Then generate `w:` lines with `_` for passing positions.

**Step 2 — Duration heuristic for residual mismatches**
Where `noteCount > syllableCount` after step 1, assign `_` to shortest-duration notes until counts balance.

**Step 3 — Assert and flag**
If still mismatched after step 2, flag the tune for manual review rather than silently mis-aligning.
