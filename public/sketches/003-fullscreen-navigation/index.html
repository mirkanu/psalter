Comparison & Recommendation

Variant Use Case Staff Visibility Nav Accessibility Scrolling
A: Sticky Bottom ★ Mobile portrait (most) Good (nav doesn't block) Excellent (no scroll) Verse text scrolls, nav stays
B: Overlay Top Mobile (portrait/landscape) Excellent (maximized) Good (overlay persistent) Full content scrolls
C: Landscape Grid Tablet (landscape) Good (side doesn't block) Excellent (always visible) Verse scrolls in main area

Recommended Strategy

  • Portrait (<768px): Use Variant A (sticky bottom). Most common, proven mobile pattern.
  • Landscape (768px-1024px): Use Variant B or C. Landscape has wider viewport, either works.
  • Desktop (>1024px): Full-screen mode less relevant (regular layout preferred), but if enabled, use Variant C equivalent with side nav.
